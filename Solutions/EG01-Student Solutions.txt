2. Users can browse nearby restaurants,
3. Users can view menus

5. Customers can estimate delivery times
6. Customers can rate their experience after delivery

8. System monitoring must be available at all times
9. System review and activity must be available at all times

11. Delivery drivers update delivery status throughout the process

13. Delivery drivers can update delivery status at order received,

Customer can browse restaurants and menus
Customer places order
Customer can view order status
Customer can view delivery times
Customers can rate their experience
Restaurants can accept or reject orders
Restaurants can mark when food is ready
Restaurants can set whether they are open or closed
Restaurants can set and modify menus and item availability
Delivery drivers can set their schedule
Delivery drivers can accept or decline delivery requests
Delivery drivers can access restaurant and customer locations and get directions
Delivery drivers can update delivery status

Non-functional

Handle incoming orders efficiently
System must handle multiple restaurants
System must handle multiple drivers
System must handle multiple customers
System must be a single platform

Re-write badly written requirements

Handle incoming orders efficiently = system should alert restaurant
with a clear auditory or visual cue of the arrival of a new order and
provide key information in a single view to allow a quick accept or
decline decision. This should be available within 10 seconds of order
placement. The system should then provide a screen or printout for the
kitchen

Rate their experience after delivery = request stars out of five for
quality, price and delivery and a space for a review
